Privacy Policy

Cloud Motion Lab · Last updated May 3, 2026

MP3 Cutter is built to trim audio and make ringtones, and to keep your files private. This page explains exactly what the app does and does not collect.

What we collect

Nothing personal. The app does not require an account. It does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or device identifiers for advertising purposes.

The audio files you import

Audio files you import into MP3 Cutter, the trim points you set, and the clips you export are stored locally on your device. They are not transmitted, stored on a server, or shared with any third party. If you delete the app, your imported files and saved exports are deleted with it.

Anonymous analytics

The app uses TelemetryDeck for anonymous, privacy-preserving usage analytics. TelemetryDeck collects coarse, non-identifying signals (such as which screen was viewed, whether an export was completed, app version, device type, country) hashed with a daily rotating salt. No persistent device identifier is stored. You can read TelemetryDeck's privacy approach at telemetrydeck.com/privacy.

In-app purchases

MP3 Cutter offers an optional Pro upgrade as either a weekly auto-renewing subscription with a free introductory trial or a one-time lifetime purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple's App Store. MP3 Cutter receives only an Apple-issued receipt confirming entitlement. We do not see your Apple ID, your payment details, or your billing address.

What we do not do

  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of any personal data
  • No background location, no contacts, no microphone recording, no camera
  • No advertising SDKs of any kind

Children

MP3 Cutter is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content. It is suitable for general use, including by children, and complies with COPPA by collecting no personal information from any user.

Changes to this policy

If we change anything material, the "Last updated" date above will move and we will note the change in the app's What's New on its next release.
